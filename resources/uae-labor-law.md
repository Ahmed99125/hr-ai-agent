# UAE Labor Law — Key Provisions for HR Operations
Authority: UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (Effective February 2, 2022)
Replaces: Federal Law No. 8 of 1980

---

## 1. Probation Period
- **Maximum**: 6 months (180 days)
- During probation: 14 days written notice required to terminate.
- Employee who leaves within probation after less than 6 months and joins another UAE employer â€” the new employer may be liable for recruitment costs if the employee absconds.

---

## 2. Work Permits & Visa
- All employees must have a valid UAE residence visa and work permit.
- Work permits are issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE).
- Medical fitness test required for visa issuance.

---

## 3. Annual Leave
- **Less than 1 year of service**: Accrued at 2.5 calendar days per month.
- **1+ year of service**: 30 calendar days per year.
- Leave can be taken in one block or split with employer agreement.
- Employer may schedule annual leave based on work requirements.

**Reference**: Article 29, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021

---

## 4. Sick Leave
- **First 15 days**: Full salary
- **Next 30 days**: Half salary
- **Remaining 45 days**: Unpaid
- Total: 90 days per year
- Valid medical certificate required.

**Reference**: Article 31, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021

---

## 5. Maternity Leave
- **Duration**: 60 days
  - First 45 days: Full salary
  - Next 15 days: Half salary
- Additional 45 days unpaid leave may be taken (for illness arising from pregnancy/birth).
- Paternity leave: 5 working days within 6 months of birth.

**Reference**: Article 30, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021

---

## 6. Working Hours
- **Standard**: 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week
- **Ramadan**: 6 hours/day for Muslim employees
- **Overtime**: First 2 hours â€” 125% of normal wage; overtime between 9 PM and 4 AM â€” 150%
- Friday (or Saturday for some sectors) is the weekly rest day.

## 9. End-of-Service Gratuity
| Years of Service | Rate per Year |
|--|--|
| First 5 years | 21 calendar days' basic salary per year |
| Beyond 5 years | 30 calendar days' basic salary per year |

- **Cap**: Maximum of 2 years' salary total.
- Calculated on basic salary only (excludes housing, transport allowances).
- Applies regardless of whether the employee resigns or is terminated (after 1 year of service).
- If terminated for gross misconduct (Article 44), employer may forfeit gratuity entitlement.

**Reference**: Article 51, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021

## 11. ILOE Insurance
- All employees must be enrolled in the ILOE (Involuntary Loss of Employment) scheme.
- Contribution: AED 5/month (employees earning under AED 16,000/month)
- Provides 3 months of salary support if involuntarily terminated.
