# Jordan Labor Law — Key Provisions for HR Operations
Authority: Jordan Labour Law No. 8 of 1996 (Amended 2008, 2010, 2019)

---

## 1. Probation Period
- **Maximum**: 3 months
- Must be agreed in writing and stated in the employment contract.
- During probation: either party may terminate without notice or compensation.

**Reference**: Article 31, Labour Law No. 8 of 1996

---

## 2. Annual Leave
- **Less than 5 years of service**: 14 working days per year
- **5+ years of service**: 21 working days per year
- Leave accrues from the first day of employment.
- Employee may carry forward a maximum of 14 days to the following year with employer agreement.

**Reference**: Article 61, Labour Law No. 8 of 1996

---

## 3. Sick Leave
- **First 14 days**: Full salary (paid sick leave)
- **Next 14 days**: Half salary
- Total: up to 28 days per year
- Valid medical certificate required.
- Employee on sick leave cannot be terminated during the sick leave period.

**Reference**: Article 65, Labour Law No. 8 of 1996

---

## 4. Maternity Leave
- **Duration**: 10 weeks (70 days) fully paid
- Can begin 4 weeks before the expected delivery date.
- Nursing break: 1 hour per day for 1 year after birth.
- Employer cannot terminate a pregnant employee or employee on maternity leave.

**Reference**: Article 70, Labour Law No. 8 of 1996

---

## 5. Working Hours
- **Standard**: 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week
- **Ramadan**: Hours reduced by 2 hours per day for Muslim employees
- **Overtime**: 125% for regular OT; 150% for Fridays and public holidays
- Weekly rest day: Friday (mandatory)

---

## 6. Public Holidays (Paid)
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Eid Al-Fitr: 3 days
- Eid Al-Adha: 3 days
- Islamic New Year
- Prophet's Birthday
- Easter (variable — for Christian employees)
- Labor Day (May 1)
- Jordan Independence Day (May 25)
- Army Day (June 10)
- Christmas (December 25)

---

## 7. Termination & Notice Period
- **Contracts of 1+ year**: 1 month written notice by either party
- **Contracts under 1 year**: Notice = working days in 1 month's notice period
- Termination without valid cause entitles employee to compensation equal to 1 month's salary per year of service (in addition to notice pay).

**Reference**: Articles 21–32, Labour Law No. 8 of 1996

---

## 8. End-of-Service Gratuity
- **Entitlement**: 1 month salary per year of service
- Minimum service: 1 year for entitlement
- Applies to both termination and resignation.

**Reference**: Article 32, Labour Law No. 8 of 1996

---

## 9. Social Security (الضمان الاجتماعي)
- **Employee contribution**: 7.5% of basic salary
- **Employer contribution**: 14.25% of basic salary
- Covers: old age pension, disability, death, workplace injuries.
- All employees under 60 working in Jordan must be registered.
