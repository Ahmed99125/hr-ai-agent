# SOP-04: Housing Allowance Policy
## بدل السكن | Housing Allowance Policy

**Applies to**: All entities (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan) — eligibility varies by grade
**Reviewed**: Annually

---

## Overview
The company provides a housing allowance (بدل السكن) as part of the compensation package for eligible employees. The allowance is intended to assist with accommodation costs in the employee's work location.

---

## Eligibility

### Who is eligible?
| Grade Level | Eligibility |
|-------------|-------------|
| Grade 1–3 (Junior) | Not eligible |
| Grade 4–6 (Mid-level) | Eligible (standard allowance) |
| Grade 7–9 (Senior) | Eligible (enhanced allowance) |
| Grade 10+ (Management) | Eligible (executive allowance or company-provided housing) |

*Grade definitions are outlined in the Job Grading Framework. Contact HR to confirm your grade.*

---

## How to Claim Housing Allowance

### For new employees:
- Housing allowance is automatically included in your offer letter and payslip if eligible.
- No separate claim is needed — it is paid monthly with salary.

### For employees who recently became eligible (promotion):
1. Your manager confirms the promotion/grade change in writing
2. HR processes the grade update in the payroll system
3. Housing allowance begins from the **first day of the new grade** (or promotion effective date)

---

## Documentation Required
To establish housing allowance eligibility (where required by law):
- **Lease contract** (عقد إيجار): Copy of your residential lease agreement
- **IBAN/Bank details**: Allowance is paid directly into your registered bank account
- **Proof of residency**: Utility bill or equivalent (where applicable)

---

## Important Notes
- **Taxability (Egypt)**: In Egypt, housing allowances above a certain threshold may be subject to income tax. Finance will advise.
- **Company-provided housing**: Some senior staff in remote or plant locations receive company-provided accommodation instead of a cash allowance. You cannot receive both.
- **Impact on gratuity**: In most entities, housing allowance is NOT included in gratuity calculations (gratuity is based on basic salary only).

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does the housing allowance increase every year?**
A: Not automatically. Allowances are reviewed annually and adjusted based on market data and performance ratings.

**Q: If I move to a more expensive accommodation, can I get a higher allowance?**
A: The allowance is fixed per grade — it does not vary by the actual cost of your specific accommodation.

**Q: What if I live with family and have no lease?**
A: The allowance is still paid as part of compensation. You do not need to prove how you spend it in most cases.

**Q: Is the housing allowance paid during leave?**
A: Yes, it is paid as part of your normal monthly payroll, including during approved leave.
