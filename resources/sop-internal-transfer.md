# SOP-03: Internal Transfer Policy
## سياسة النقل الداخلي | Internal Transfer Policy

**Applies to**: All entities (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan)
**Processing Time**: 2–4 weeks
**Approvals Required**: Current Manager + Receiving Manager + HR

---

## What is an Internal Transfer?
An internal transfer (نقل داخلي) is the movement of an employee from one department, location, or entity within the company to another. Transfers can be:
- **Lateral**: Same job grade, different department or location
- **Promotional**: Higher job grade in a new department
- **Cross-entity**: Transfer between KSA, UAE, Egypt, or Jordan entities

---

## Types of Transfers

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Voluntary** | Employee-initiated request to move to a different role/location |
| **Operational** | Company-initiated based on business needs |
| **Cross-entity** | Involves a change in employment contract and country |

---

## Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for a voluntary internal transfer, an employee must:
- Have completed **at least 12 months** in their current role
- Have a satisfactory performance rating (no active PIPs)
- Have no outstanding disciplinary actions
- Obtain approval from their **current line manager**

*Exceptions apply for operational transfers initiated by the company.*

---

## Process

### Step 1 — Employee Expresses Interest
- Speak with your current line manager about your interest in transferring
- Or contact HR to inquire about open internal positions

### Step 2 — Submit Transfer Request
- Complete the Internal Transfer Request Form (available from HR)
- Or submit the request via this HR Agent

### Step 3 — Current Manager Review (5 business days)
- Current manager approves or declines based on team needs and transition feasibility
- If approved, provides a proposed transition timeline (usually 4–8 weeks)

### Step 4 — Receiving Manager Interview
- Receiving manager conducts a brief interview (may be informal)
- Receiving manager approves or declines the transfer

### Step 5 — HR Review and Processing
- HR validates eligibility, checks headcount approvals, and updates employment records
- For cross-entity transfers: HR prepares a new employment contract for the destination country

### Step 6 — Offer and Confirmation
- Employee receives a written transfer confirmation letter outlining:
  - New role, department, and location
  - New salary (if changed)
  - Effective date
  - Transfer allowances (if applicable)

### Step 7 — Transition Period
- Standard transition: **4 weeks** in current role to hand over
- Longer transitions may be agreed for critical roles

---

## Cross-Entity Transfer (International)
When moving between KSA, UAE, Egypt, and Jordan:
- A new employment contract for the destination country is issued
- Old contract is terminated (gratuity may be settled or transferred — HR will advise)
- A new work visa/permit must be obtained for the destination country
- The employee must complete a new onboarding process for the entity

---

## Transfer Allowances
Employees transferred due to operational/company needs may be entitled to:
- Relocation allowance (one-time, covers moving costs — amount per grade)
- Temporary accommodation (up to 3 months) in the new location
- Family relocation support (if applicable and grade-eligible)

*Voluntary transfers generally do not include relocation allowances.*

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can my manager block my transfer request?**
A: Your manager's approval is part of the process, but HR can facilitate discussions if there is a legitimate business impact concern.

**Q: Does my service continuity reset on transfer?**
A: For transfers within the same entity, service is continuous. For cross-entity transfers, it depends on the agreement — HR will specify in the confirmation letter.

**Q: Will my salary change on transfer?**
A: Not necessarily. Lateral transfers usually maintain the same salary. Promotional transfers include a salary increase per the salary matrix. Cross-entity transfers may reflect local market rates.
