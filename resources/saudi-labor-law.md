# Saudi Labor Law — Key Provisions for HR Operations
Last Updated: 2025 | Authority: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD)
Also referred to as: نظام العمل السعودي | Nitham Al-Amal

---

## 1. Employment Contract (عقد العمل)
- Employment contracts must be in writing, in Arabic, and signed by both parties.
- Fixed-term contracts: automatically renewed if continued without objection.
- Indefinite contracts: common for full-time employees.

---

## 2. Probation Period (فترة التجربة)
- **Standard**: 90 days (3 months)
- **Maximum**: 180 days (6 months) — only with written mutual agreement
- Either party may terminate during probation without notice or compensation.
- Cannot be set more than once for the same employee.

**Reference**: Article 53, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 3. Annual Leave (الإجازة السنوية)
- **Less than 5 years of service**: 21 calendar days per year
- **5 or more years of service**: 30 calendar days per year
- Leave cannot be forfeited; unused leave must be compensated in cash upon termination.
- Employee may carry forward up to half their annual entitlement with employer's consent.

**Reference**: Article 109, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 4. Sick Leave (الإجازة المرضية)
- **First 30 days**: Full salary
- **Next 60 days**: Three-quarters (75%) salary
- **Next 30 days**: Unpaid (or per company policy)
- Medical certificate required after 3 consecutive sick days.
- Employer cannot terminate employee during sick leave period.

**Reference**: Article 117, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 5. Maternity Leave (إجازة الأمومة)
- **Duration**: 10 weeks (70 days) fully paid
- May begin up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date.
- Additional unpaid leave of 1 month may be requested.

**Reference**: Article 151, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 6. Hajj Leave (إجازة الحج)
- **Duration**: 10 days paid
- Granted once per employment period (not renewable)
- Requires 2 years of continuous service with the same employer.

**Reference**: Article 114, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 7. Working Hours (ساعات العمل)
- **Standard**: 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week
- **Ramadan (رمضان)**: 6 hours/day, 36 hours/week for Muslim employees
- **Overtime**: 150% of normal wage (time-and-a-half)
- **Friday**: Official weekend — no work (with exceptions for shift workers)

**Reference**: Articles 98–107, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 8. Public Holidays (الإجازات الرسمية)
- **Eid Al-Fitr (عيد الفطر)**: 4 days
- **Eid Al-Adha (عيد الأضحى)**: 4 days
- **Saudi National Day (اليوم الوطني)**: September 23 — 1 day
- **Founding Day (يوم التأسيس)**: February 22 — 1 day

---

## 9. Termination & Notice Period (إنهاء العقد والإشعار)
- **Indefinite contracts**: 60 days written notice by either party
- **Fixed-term contracts**: Cannot be terminated before expiry without compensation unless mutual agreement
- Employer may terminate without notice for gross misconduct (Article 80).
- Employee may resign without notice if employer violates the contract (Article 81).

**Reference**: Articles 74–80, Saudi Labor Law

---

## 10. End-of-Service Gratuity (مكافأة نهاية الخدمة)

### Article 84 — Termination / Contract End / Mutual Agreement
| Years of Service | Rate per Year |
|--|--|
| First 5 years | ½ month salary per year |
| Beyond 5 years | 1 month salary per year |

### Article 85 — Resignation
| Years of Service | Percentage of Art. 84 Amount |
|--|--|
| Less than 2 years | 0% (no gratuity) |
| 2–5 years | ⅓ (one-third) |
| 5–10 years | ⅔ (two-thirds) |
| 10+ years | 100% (full amount) |

*Gratuity is calculated on the last basic salary (excluding housing, transport, and other allowances).*

---

## 11. Saudization / Nitaqat (نطاقات)
- All private sector companies must meet specific Saudi national quotas.
- Companies are categorized: Platinum → Green → Yellow → Red based on compliance.
- **Red** companies face restrictions: no new work visas, no license renewals.
- Saudization percentages vary by industry; agriculture and domestic workers are exempt.
- The "Saudi" count includes Saudi nationals on GOSI-registered payrolls.

---

## 12. Wage Protection System (نظام حماية الأجور - WPS)
- All private sector employees must be paid through WPS-registered accounts.
- Salaries must be paid within 10 days of the due date.
- Non-compliance triggers fines and visa suspensions.

---

## 13. GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance)
- Both employer and employee must register with GOSI.
- **Employee contribution**: 9.75% of salary
- **Employer contribution**: 11.75% of salary
- Contributions cover retirement, disability, and workplace injury compensation.
