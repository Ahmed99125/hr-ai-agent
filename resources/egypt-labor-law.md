# Egypt Labor Law — Key Provisions for HR Operations
Authority: Egypt Labour Law No. 12 of 2003
Supplemented by: Social Insurance Law No. 148 of 2019

---

## 1. Probation Period
- **Maximum**: 3 months
- Must be explicitly stated in the employment contract in writing.
- Cannot be applied more than once for the same employee.

**Reference**: Article 28, Labour Law No. 12 of 2003

---

## 2. Annual Leave
- **Less than 10 years of service**: 21 working days per year
- **10+ years of service**: 30 working days per year
- Employees over 50 years old or with a disability: 30 working days regardless of tenure
- Unused leave cannot be forfeited; must be compensated upon termination.

**Reference**: Article 47, Labour Law No. 12 of 2003

---

## 3. Sick Leave
- Sick leave is administered through the Social Insurance Authority (NOSI).
- **First 3 months**: Full pay (75% from social insurance + employer top-up)
- **Next 3 months**: 75% pay
- A maximum of 6 months per year.
- Valid medical certificate required from a recognized physician.

**Reference**: Article 54, Labour Law No. 12 of 2003

---

## 4. Maternity Leave
- **Duration**: 90 days paid (for 3 or fewer children)
- Must have completed 10 months with the same employer before taking leave.
- Additional unpaid leave may be taken for up to 2 years per child (career break).
- Nursing break: 2 nursing breaks per day (minimum 30 minutes each) for 2 years after birth.

**Reference**: Article 91, Labour Law No. 12 of 2003

---

## 5. Working Hours
- **Standard**: 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week
- Break of at least 1 hour included in working hours (not counted in effective working time)
- **Ramadan**: Working hours reduced by 1 hour for Muslim employees
- **Overtime**: 135% for daytime OT; 170% for night or Friday OT

---

## 6. Public Holidays (Paid)
- Egyptian National Day (January 25) — Revolution Day
- Sinai Liberation Day (April 25)
- Labor Day (May 1)
- June 30 Revolution Day
- Army Day (October 6)
- National Unity Day (October 24)
- Eid Al-Fitr: 3 days
- Eid Al-Adha: 3 days
- Islamic New Year
- Prophet's Birthday
- Christmas (January 7) — for Christian employees

---

## 7. Termination & Notice Period
- **Indefinite contracts**: 2 months written notice (by either party)
- Termination without valid reason entitles employee to compensation (Article 122)
- Employee terminated without cause receives: 2 months' salary per year of service

**Reference**: Articles 110–124, Labour Law No. 12 of 2003

---

## 8. End-of-Service Gratuity
- **First 5 years of service**: ½ month salary per year
- **Beyond 5 years**: 1 month salary per year
- Minimum service for entitlement: 1 year
- Gratuity is separate from social insurance pension.

**Reference**: Article 54, Labour Law No. 12 of 2003

---

## 9. Social Insurance (التأمينات الاجتماعية)
- **Employee contribution**: 11% of salary (capped salary ceiling applies)
- **Employer contribution**: 19% of salary
- Covers: retirement pension, disability, workplace injury, death, and unemployment benefits.
- All employees must be registered from the first day of employment.
