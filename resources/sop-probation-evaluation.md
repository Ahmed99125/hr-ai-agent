# SOP-06: Probation Period Evaluation Process
## تقييم فترة التجربة | Probation Evaluation Process

**Applies to**: All new hires across all entities
**Timeline**: 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day checkpoints

---

## Overview
All new employees serve a probation period during which their performance, cultural fit, and role suitability are assessed. The probation evaluation is a structured process with defined milestones.

---

## Probation Periods by Country
| Country | Standard Probation | Maximum Probation | Legal Reference |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| KSA | 90 days | 180 days (with written consent) | Saudi Labor Law, Art. 53 |
| UAE | Up to 180 days | 180 days | UAE Decree-Law No. 33, Art. 9 |
| Egypt | 90 days | 90 days | Egypt Labour Law, Art. 28 |
| Jordan | 90 days | 90 days | Jordan Labour Law, Art. 31 |

---

## Evaluation Milestones

### 30-Day Check-In
- **Purpose**: Early identification of onboarding issues or role misalignment
- **Conducted by**: Direct line manager (informally)
- **Topics covered**:
  - How is the employee settling in?
  - Are they able to perform core tasks independently?
  - Any training gaps or tools/access issues?
  - Questions or concerns from the employee?
- **HR action**: Review onboarding checklist completion

### 60-Day Review
- **Purpose**: Mid-probation formal assessment
- **Conducted by**: Line manager with HR participation (if needed)
- **Format**: Structured 1:1 meeting using the 60-Day Review form
- **Assessment areas**:
  - Technical competency (role-specific tasks)
  - Professionalism and attitude
  - Teamwork and collaboration
  - Attendance and punctuality
  - Alignment with company values
- **Outcome**: "On Track", "Needs Improvement", or "At Risk"

### 90-Day Final Evaluation
- **Purpose**: Final decision on confirmation of employment
- **Conducted by**: Line manager + department head, with HR
- **Format**: Formal review meeting + written evaluation form
- **Decision options**:
  1. **Confirm Employment** — Employee passes probation, regular employment begins
  2. **Extend Probation** — Allowed only in KSA (up to 90 additional days), requires written mutual agreement
  3. **Terminate Employment** — If performance is unsatisfactory; HR processes termination in compliance with local law

---

## Extending Probation (KSA Only)
- Extension must be in writing and signed by both employee and employer
- Extension cannot exceed a total probation of 180 days
- Cannot extend probation more than once
- Employee must be informed in writing before the original 90-day period ends

---

## Confirming Employment
When the employee passes probation:
1. Manager submits the completed evaluation form to HR
2. HR issues a **Confirmation of Employment Letter** (خطاب تثبيت)
3. Employee's status in the system is updated to "Confirmed"
4. Employee becomes eligible for all confirmed employee benefits (e.g., annual leave accrual in some plans)

---

## Termination During Probation
- Either party may terminate without notice or end-of-service gratuity during probation (KSA, Egypt, Jordan)
- UAE: 14 days' written notice required
- Employee must receive all unpaid wages and accrued leave up to termination date
- HR must process the termination in the system within 5 business days

---

## Performance Improvement During Probation
If an employee shows a "Needs Improvement" rating at 60 days:
1. Manager documents specific performance concerns
2. A **mini Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** is created for the remaining 30 days
3. HR provides support (coaching, training resources)
4. Final evaluation at 90 days determines confirmation or termination

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does annual leave accrue during probation?**
A: Leave accrues from day 1 in most countries but may only be taken after confirmation in some entity policies. HR will advise.

**Q: Can I request a transfer during probation?**
A: Generally no — you must complete probation in your current role first.

**Q: What happens if I'm sick during probation?**
A: Sick leave during probation is handled as normal (medical certificate required). The probation period does not automatically extend due to sick leave.

**Q: Can I resign during probation?**
A: Yes, without notice in most countries (except UAE: 14 days required). Check your employment contract.
