# SOP-02: Exit and Re-Entry Visa Process
## تأشيرة الخروج والعودة | Exit & Re-Entry Visa (KSA)

**Applies to**: Expatriate employees in Saudi Arabia (KSA entity only)
**Processing Time**: 3–7 business days (Absher / Muqeem)
**Managed via**: Absher Platform (أبشر) + MHRSD

---

## What is an Exit & Re-Entry Visa?
An exit and re-entry visa (تأشيرة خروج وعودة) allows expatriate employees on a Saudi Iqama (residence permit) to leave Saudi Arabia and return within the visa's validity period.

**Types**:
- **Single Exit & Re-Entry (خروج وعودة مفردة)**: Allows one departure and one return
- **Multiple Exit & Re-Entry (خروج وعودة متعددة)**: Allows multiple departures and returns within the validity period

---

## Eligibility Requirements
- Must have a valid Iqama (residence permit)
- Iqama must not expire before the intended return date
- No outstanding violations or legal holds on your Iqama
- Employer must not have placed a "no exit" restriction
- Must not have absconded (بلاغ تغيب) report against you

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1 — Request HR Authorization
- Notify HR at least **5 business days before** your intended travel date
- Inform HR of: travel dates, destination country, type of visa required (single/multiple)

### Step 2 — HR Processes via Absher / Muqeem
HR or your Company PRO (Public Relations Officer) will:
1. Log in to **Absher Business (أبشر أعمال)** or **Muqeem (مقيم)**
2. Navigate to "Exit & Re-Entry Visa" service
3. Enter your Iqama number and travel details
4. Pay the visa fee (deducted from company account or employee as per policy)
5. Submit the application

### Step 3 — Approval
- Standard processing: **3–5 business days**
- Urgent processing: **24–48 hours** (higher fee applies)
- You will receive an SMS confirmation on your registered Saudi mobile number

### Step 4 — Print / Digital Visa
- Download and print the visa confirmation from Absher
- Keep a copy on your phone for airport presentation
- Note: the visa is linked to your Iqama — border control can verify electronically

## Important Notes
- **Iqama expiry**: If your Iqama expires while you are outside Saudi Arabia, you cannot return until the Iqama is renewed (which requires you to be present). Ensure your Iqama is valid before traveling.
- **Family visas**: If your family is under your sponsorship (kafala), they also need exit/re-entry visas.
- **Final exit visa (تأشيرة خروج نهائي)**: If you are leaving Saudi Arabia permanently (resignation, end of contract), you need a final exit visa. Do NOT use an exit/re-entry visa if you do not plan to return.
- **Runway time**: Always allow buffer; immigration systems can have processing delays.

Some nationalities require Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) clearance before issuance. HR will advise if this applies to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does an exit/re-entry visa remain valid?**
A: Single: 6 months from issue date. Multiple: 6 or 12 months depending on the type issued.

**Q: What if I overstay my exit/re-entry visa abroad?**
A: The visa will be cancelled. You would need an emergency re-entry visa and may face fines.

**Q: Can I travel immediately after submitting the application?**
A: No — you must wait for approval before traveling. Attempting to exit without a valid visa may result in detention.

**Q: What if I need to travel urgently before the visa is processed?**
A: Contact HR immediately. Urgent processing (24–48 hrs) can be requested with manager approval.
