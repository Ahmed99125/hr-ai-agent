# Nitaqat / Saudization — Compliance Guide
Authority: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD)
Also known as: نطاقات — Nationalization Quota System

---

## What is Nitaqat?
Nitaqat (نطاقات) is Saudi Arabia's Saudization quota system requiring all private sector employers to hire a minimum percentage of Saudi nationals based on industry and company size.

---

## How Nitaqat Works
Companies are color-coded based on their Saudi workforce percentage:

| Band | Description | Implications |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Platinum (بلاتيني)** | Exceeds target significantly | Full benefits, priority processing, can transfer visas freely |
| **High Green (أخضر مرتفع)** | Well above target | Very good standing |
| **Medium Green (أخضر متوسط)** | Above target | Good standing |
| **Low Green (أخضر منخفض)** | At or just above target | Good standing, can recruit expatriates |
| **Yellow (أصفر)** | Below target | Cannot recruit new expatriates, limited services |
| **Red (أحمر)** | Well below target | Cannot renew or issue work visas, risk of sanctions |

## Saudization Rates by Sector (Examples)
| Sector | Minimum Saudi % |
|--------|----------------|
| Insurance | 40% |
| Banking & Finance | 60% |
| Retail (large) | 70% |
| Engineering firms | 10–15% |
| IT companies | 10% |
| Industrial/Manufacturing | 6–15% (varies by size) |
| Oil & Gas | 15–35% |
| Construction | 5–6% |

---

## Who Counts as "Saudi" in Nitaqat?
- Saudi nationals registered in the company's GOSI file
- Saudi wives of non-Saudi husbands (counted if employed)
- Children of Saudi mothers (born to non-Saudi father, if Saudi citizenship granted)
- **NOT counted**: Saudis not registered in GOSI, unpaid internships, part-time below threshold

---

## Exemptions from Nitaqat
- Companies with fewer than 6 total employees
- Domestic workers
- Agricultural workers
- Companies in specific free zones (e.g., NEOM, certain KAEC zones)

---

## Penalties for Non-Compliance (Red Band)
- Suspension of new work visa issuance
- Suspension of existing visa renewals
- No new commercial license activities
- Priority in Saudization audits and labor inspections

---

## Key Compliance Tips
1. Register all Saudi employees in GOSI promptly.
2. Ensure Saudi employees are on the correct salary (WPS must reflect actual salary).
3. Use the "Mu'aqaba" platform to check company Nitaqat status.
4. Apply for Nitaqat exemptions if eligible (seasonal work, disability, etc.).
5. Partner with TAQAT (Hadaf) for recruitment subsidies when hiring Saudi nationals.
