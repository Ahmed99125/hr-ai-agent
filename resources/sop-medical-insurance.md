# SOP-05: Medical Insurance Coverage
## التأمين الطبي | Medical Insurance Policy

**Applies to**: All entities (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan)
**Insurance Provider**: Varies by entity (HR will confirm current provider)

---

## Overview
All permanent employees and their eligible dependents are covered under the company's group medical insurance plan. Coverage begins from the **first day of employment**.

---

## Who is Covered?
| Member | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| Employee | Full coverage (Class A or B depending on grade) |
| Spouse | Covered (declaration required within 30 days of marriage or joining) |
| Children (up to age 18, or 21 if in full-time education) | Covered |
| Domestic workers | Not covered under group plan |

## What is Covered?

### Included:
- General practitioner consultations
- Specialist consultations (with referral if required by plan)
- Emergency room visits
- Hospitalization and surgery
- Diagnostic tests (blood work, X-rays, MRI — within limits)
- Prescription medications (approved formulary)
- Maternity care (from 10 months of coverage for planned pregnancies)
- Dental: Basic cleaning and fillings (up to plan limits)
- Vision: Annual eye exam + glasses or contact lens allowance

### Excluded (generally):
- Cosmetic procedures
- Fertility treatments (unless mandated by local law)
- Weight loss surgery
- Pre-existing conditions (waived after 12 months of continuous coverage)
- Treatment abroad (except emergency)
- Experimental treatments

## How to Use Your Insurance

### Step 1 — Get Your Insurance Card
- Issued during onboarding (physical card + digital copy via insurer's app)
- Contact HR if your card has not been received within 2 weeks of joining

### Step 2 — Visit a Network Provider
- Use the insurer's app or website to find in-network clinics and hospitals
- Present your insurance card at reception — direct billing applies for most providers
- For specialist consultations: a referral from a GP may be required (plan-dependent)

### Step 3 — Pre-Authorization (for planned procedures)
- Hospitalizations, surgeries, and high-cost procedures require **pre-authorization**
- Pre-auth request is submitted by the treating hospital/clinic on your behalf
- Approval is usually within 24–48 hours

### Step 4 — Claims (for out-of-network or abroad emergency)
- Retain all original receipts and medical reports
- Submit claim form + documents to the insurance portal within **60 days** of treatment
- Reimbursement usually processed within 15–21 business days

---

## Adding/Removing Dependents
- New spouse: Notify HR within **30 days** of marriage (marriage certificate required)
- New child: Notify HR within **30 days** of birth (birth certificate required)
- Late additions may not be covered retroactively

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What should I do in a medical emergency?**
A: Go to the nearest emergency room. Present your insurance card. Emergency treatment is covered even at out-of-network facilities. Notify HR/insurer within 48 hours.

**Q: Is dental and vision covered?**
A: Basic dental and annual vision exam are covered. Orthodontics (braces) and corrective surgery (LASIK) are generally excluded.

**Q: Can I use my insurance in another country while on work travel?**
A: Emergency treatment abroad is covered. Routine care abroad is not. Keep receipts for reimbursement claims.

**Q: What happens to my coverage when I leave the company?**
A: Coverage ends on your last working day. You may be eligible for optional continuation coverage — contact HR before your last day.
