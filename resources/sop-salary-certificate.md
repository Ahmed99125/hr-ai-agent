# SOP-01: Salary Certificate Issuance
## خطاب التعريف بالراتب | Salary Certificate / Employment Letter

**Valid for**: All entities (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan)
**Processing Time**: 3 business days
**Issued by**: HR Department

---

## What is a Salary Certificate?
A salary certificate (خطاب تعريف بالراتب) is an official letter from the company confirming an employee's:
- Full name and job title
- Department and company name
- Employment start date
- Monthly salary (basic + allowances, or basic only as requested)
- Employment status (permanent / fixed-term)

It is commonly required for: bank loans, credit cards, visa applications, rental agreements, school enrollment, and government services.

---

## How to Request a Salary Certificate

### Step 1 — Submit a Request
- Submit your request through this HR Agent (say: "I need a salary certificate")
- Or email: hr@company.com with subject "Salary Certificate Request"
- Or submit a physical form at the HR office

### Step 2 — Specify the Purpose
Let HR know the purpose of the certificate so the correct format is used:
- **Bank/Finance**: Shows detailed breakdown of basic salary + allowances
- **General/Visa**: Shows total gross salary
- **Arabic version**: Available on request for government entities
- **Bilingual (AR + EN)**: Standard format

### Step 3 — Verification
HR verifies your employment details in the system (typically same day)

### Step 4 — Issuance
- Certificate is prepared, signed by HR Manager, and stamped with company seal
- Usually ready within **3 business days**

### Step 5 — Collection
- **Email delivery**: Scan of signed certificate sent to your work email
- **Physical pickup**: Collect from HR office (bring your ID)

---

## Important Notes
- The certificate reflects your salary as of the **date of issue**.
- A new certificate must be requested if your salary changes.
- Salary certificates are confidential — do not share with unauthorized parties.
- For KSA: certificates can be apostilled (توثيق) through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs if needed for international use.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long is a salary certificate valid?**
A: Most banks and institutions accept certificates issued within **3 months**. Check with the requesting party.

**Q: Can I get a salary certificate in Arabic?**
A: Yes. Request an Arabic version when submitting your request.

**Q: What if I need it urgently (same day)?**
A: Urgent requests can be processed within 4–8 hours for an additional approval from the HR Manager. Contact HR directly.

**Q: Can a salary certificate show a higher amount than my actual salary?**
A: No. The certificate must reflect the actual salary on record. Falsifying this is a serious violation.
